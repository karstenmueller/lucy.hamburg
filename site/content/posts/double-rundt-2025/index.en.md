A Co32 battle flag was recently spotted flying the evening before the Double Rundt race, where a sailor completed the 32-nautical-mile course solo.

While the race is typically double-handed, the sailor onboard "Lucy" navigated the course alone. Photos taken in Langballigau Harbour after the race show the Co32 battle flag displayed on the boat.

"It was pretty breezy, and with the No2 genoa and full main, I had a bit too much sail up," the commented Karsten. "But Lucy kept me safe, and I finished in good time."  Good to see.
